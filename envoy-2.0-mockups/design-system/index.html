Linen

A calm, editorial system for wedding planners. Warm paper, deep ink, and a single clay accent — the colour of a wax seal on an invitation.

Principles

Calm is a feature.

Planners live in urgency. The interface stays quiet so the four things that matter are loud. Clay is reserved for what needs a human now.

Editorial, then operational.

Fraunces sets the emotional register — names, dates, big numbers. Geist does the work: tables, labels, controls.

Borders before shadows.

Hairlines define structure. Shadows only appear for things that float: menus, toasts, dragged cards.

Do
  • Use one accent (clay) for urgency, selection, and the primary story beat.
  • Use planner language: milestone, checklist item, hold, “50 weeks out”.
  • Right-align and tabular-figure every number.
Don't
  • Gradients, glass, neon, or rainbow charts.
  • Jira vocabulary: epic, story points, sprint velocity.
  • Chat as the home screen.

Color

Neutrals carry 95% of the surface. Clay is the only hue. Semantic colours are muted cousins that never compete with it.

Typography

Fraunces (variable, optical size 9–144) for display. Geist for UI. Geist Mono for tokens, IDs and dense numbers. Display uses light weights at large sizes and tightens tracking as it grows.

Spacing

A 4px base. Cards pad at 24, tables at 16 horizontal / 12 vertical, sections breathe at 40–48.
